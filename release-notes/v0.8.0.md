**KSP's faces and head movements, seated idles and the floating pose.**

- **Faces:** the Kerbals' faces are blended from KSP's own face clips, 21 per Kerbal, by how excited or afraid the Kerbal is. The mouth, eyelids and teeth move as they do in KSP, the female Kerbals included.
- **Head movements:** a seated Kerbal looks around slowly while idle, and quickly when excited or afraid, with KSP's 25 head clips. They keep their eyes as KSP animates them, and do not follow the camera.
- **Seated idles:** a seated Kerbal rests with KSP's slow head-bobbing loop and now and then plays one of its idle clips, such as dancing or drumming on the controls: six for the male Kerbals and five for the female ones. KSP's own female foot-stretch clip is empty and would leave her in a T pose, so it is left out.
- **Floating:** a Kerbal in free space with RCS off floats in KSP's pose, and the arms move when RCS is turned on or off.
- **Eyes:** fixed a bug that turned the male Kerbals' pupils by almost 180 degrees in some head clips.
- All of this needs the optional [Character Manager](https://github.com/paulo-santana/KSA-Character-Manager) 0.5.0 or later, which plays it. Without it, nothing changes.
- The download grows from about 14 MB to about 17 MB.
