**KSP's idle animations:** Kerbals standing around now look around, stretch and fidget now and then, with KSP's own idle variants. That's seven for the male Kerbals and seven for the female ones.

- This needs the optional [Character Manager](https://github.com/paulo-santana/KSA-Character-Manager) 0.3.0 or later, which plays them: KSA itself has no idle variants on the ground. Without it, nothing changes.
- The download grows by about 2 MB.
