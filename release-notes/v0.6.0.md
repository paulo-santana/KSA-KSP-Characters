**KSP's idle and ladder animations.**

- **Idle variety:** Kerbals standing around now look around, stretch and fidget now and then, with KSP's own idle variants: seven for the male Kerbals and seven for the female ones.
- **Ladders:** Kerbals hold KSP's ladder pose when they stop, climb down with KSP's climb-down animation instead of climbing up in reverse, and climb smoothly instead of freezing.
- This needs the optional [Character Manager](https://github.com/paulo-santana/KSA-Character-Manager) 0.3.0 or later, which plays them: KSA itself has no idle variants on the ground and only one ladder clip. Without it, nothing changes.
- The download grows by about 2 MB.
