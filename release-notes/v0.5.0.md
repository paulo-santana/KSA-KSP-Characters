**Jebediah, Bill, Bob and Valentina Kerman** join every new game, in KSP's orange suit, the way they start every career in KSP.

- This needs the optional [Character Manager](https://github.com/paulo-santana/KSA-Character-Manager) 0.2.0 or later. On its own, KSP Characters can't add crew, and nothing changes.
- They join right after the game's own Hunter, Banjo and Polaris. In a Testing start, Jeb and Bill start seated in the starting vehicles.
- Saves that already exist are not changed: the four only join new games.
