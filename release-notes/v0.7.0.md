**KSP's jetpack, and the RCS stance.**

- **KSP's jetpack:** Kerbals now wear KSP's own jetpack instead of KSA's, which was too big for them and clipped through the body. Its control arms swing out when RCS is turned on and fold back when it is turned off.
- **RCS stance:** with RCS on, Kerbals stand, walk and jump with their hands on the controls, using KSP's jetpack animations. Turning RCS on or off while standing plays KSP's animation of reaching for the arms or putting them away.
- **RCS puffs:** the thruster puffs come from the KSP jetpack's own thrusters. Only the visuals move: the thrust is the game's own, so the jetpack flies exactly as before.
- The stance, the toggle animations, the arm timing and the puffs need the optional [Character Manager](https://github.com/paulo-santana/KSA-Character-Manager) 0.4.0 or later. Without it, Kerbals still wear KSP's jetpack, and its arms still move with RCS.
- The download shrinks from about 24 MB to about 14 MB, since KSA's jetpack is no longer copied in.
